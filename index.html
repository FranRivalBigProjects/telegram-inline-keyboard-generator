Callback Data vs URL Buttons

Buttons with callback_data trigger a CallbackQuery your bot handles in code, while url buttons simply open a link. Rows are defined by grouping buttons together; each row here corresponds to one line of buttons in the keyboard.

Client-side tool. Nothing you enter here is stored or sent anywhere.
